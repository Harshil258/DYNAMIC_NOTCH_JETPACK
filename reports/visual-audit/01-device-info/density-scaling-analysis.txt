=== REALME 8 DENSITY & SCALING ROOT-CAUSE ANALYSIS ===
Device: Realme 8 (RMX3085), Android 13 (SDK 33)
Physical Resolution: 1080 x 2400 px
Physical Density: 480 dpi (density = 3.0, font_scale = 1.0)
Logical Viewport: 360 x 800 dp

1. IS THE "OVERSIZED" UI CAUSED BY ANDROID OS DISPLAY ZOOM?
Answer: NO.
Evidence:
- `adb shell settings get system font_scale` returns `1.0` (Standard 100% typography scale).
- `adb shell wm density` returns `Physical density: 480` (Factory un-modified display density).
- Android OS display size is set to the default index (no display scaling or large text enabled at OS level).

2. IS THE "OVERSIZED" UI CAUSED BY COMPOSE DENSITY MISMATCH?
Answer: NO.
Evidence:
- Jetpack Compose reads LocalDensity as `Density(3.0f, 1.0f)`.
- 1dp translates to exactly 3.0px. 1sp translates to exactly 3.0px.
- Pixel coordinates align perfectly with physical screen pixels without fractional rounding distortion.

3. ROOT CAUSE: INTERNAL APP DESIGN TOKENS AND HARCODED LAYOUTS
The perception of oversized, crowded, and misaligned UI is caused 100% by internal codebase styling tokens:

A. Dynamic Island Pill Dimensions:
- Apple Dynamic Island Compact Height: 36.67dp (110px @ 3.0x density).
- Aurora AppIslandTokens Compact Height: 48.0dp to 52.7dp (158px @ 3.0x density).
- Disparity: +16.03dp (+43.7% taller than reference).
- Compact Minimum Width: 173dp (519px) vs Apple 126dp (378px) (+37.3% wider).
- This causes the compact island to look like a bulky pill rather than a sleek, integrated notch HUD.

B. Button Dimensions & Padding:
- AppButton standard height: 56dp (168px).
- Internal button padding: horizontal 24dp, vertical 16dp.
- Text size: 16sp (48px) with bold weights.
- On a 360dp wide screen, two 56dp buttons stacked or placed horizontally consume massive vertical/horizontal real estate.

C. Card Containers & Spacing:
- Card padding: 16dp to 20dp.
- Vertical item gap: 12dp to 16dp.
- Bottom dock reservation: 92dp (`BottomDockInset = 92.dp`).
- Top bar height: 56dp.
- Total fixed non-content vertical overhead: 56dp (top) + 92dp (bottom dock) + 36.67dp (status bar) = 184.67dp.
- Available content viewport: 800 - 184.67 = 615.33dp.
- Heavy card padding in this 615dp space forces excessive scrolling and creates visual bloat.

D. Hardware Cutout Disalignment:
- Realme 8 punch-hole is physically located at top-left: bounds `[-1.33, 0, 51.33, 36.67] dp`.
- The Dynamic Island is centered horizontally at `x = 180dp` (center of 360dp width).
- Because the physical camera is on the far left, a centered black island floats in empty screen space while the punch-hole sits completely outside it, making the island feel disconnected and visually intrusive.
